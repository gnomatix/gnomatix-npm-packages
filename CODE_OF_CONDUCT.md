# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## 🟢 The Basic DOs:
* **Be informed.**
* **Be competent.**
* **Be pro-social.**
* **Be collaborative.**
* **Be service-oriented.**
* **Be sure to triple-check.**
* **Be loud** when you need to
* **Be quiet** when you need to.
* **Be aware** when you make mistakes.
* **Be aware** when they make mistakes.
* **Be first-in** to help fix the mistakes.
* **Be assured** that everyone makes mistakes.

## 🔴 The Basic DON'Ts:
* **Don't be lazy.**
* **Don't be racist.**
* **Don't be sexist.**
* **Don't be stupid.**
* **Don't forget to wipe.**
* **Don't be late for dinner.**
* **Don't forget to tip your waiter.**
* **Don't fight people**, fight robots.
